## research: d747a85d-d6a2-45d0-baed-634a99fc4d25

- **short_description**: FedSP-PEFT estimates agile story points across projects while keeping each project’s issue data local.
- **problem**: Proprietary issue data prevents software teams from pooling training data.
- **approach**: Projects train lightweight CodeBERT adapters locally, with federated learning and personalized prediction heads.
- **significance**: Personalized heads match local-only training, with about 494× less estimated upload volume than full-model federation.
- **full_description**: The framework combines a frozen CodeBERT encoder, FFA-LoRA adapters, FedProx local training, an ordinal CORN head, and project-specific prediction heads.
